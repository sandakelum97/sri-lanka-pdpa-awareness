# Commencement Status of the PDPA — as at 31 October 2026

Version 1.1 · Sources: ACT-01, ACT-02, GAZ-01 to GAZ-04, CIR-02, WEB-10 (see Legal Document Register). The DPA's own Data Protection Statement (WEB-10, updated 20 Aug 2026) independently confirms the position in §2: ss.2–3, Parts I and III from 1 Jan 2027; Parts II and VII not in force. Re-verify this file against https://www.dpa.gov.lk/guidelines.php before every content release.

## 1. How commencement works

- Original s.1(2): section 1 came into operation on the Speaker's certificate — 19 Mar 2022.
- Original s.1(3)–(5) set windows for the Minister's commencement Orders (18–36 months for most Parts; 24–48 months for Part IV; Part V no later than the s.1(3) date).
- Amendment Act s.2(1) repealed s.1(3)–(5) and substituted a new s.1(3): "All other provisions of this Act except this section, shall come into operation on such date or dates as the Minister may appoint, by Order published in the Gazette." There is now no statutory deadline and provisions may be commenced one at a time.
- Amendment Act s.2(2): anything duly done under Parts V, VI, VIII, IX and X before the amendment remains valid.
- The Amendment Act contains no commencement clause of its own, so its amendments took effect on certification, 30 Oct 2025 (Article 79 of the Constitution — confirm with a legal reviewer; see Open Questions OQ-02). Where the Amendment Act amends a Part that is not yet in operation, the amended text is what will come into force.

## 3. Plain-language summary for the website (draft wording, to be legally reviewed)

- The law exists, but most of the rules that affect ordinary people and businesses are not yet in force.
- The regulator (the Data Protection Authority) has existed since 2023.
- From 1 January 2027, the core duties for anyone who handles personal data (Part I) and the duties for controllers and processors such as DPOs, breach notification, impact assessments and overseas transfers (Part III) apply.
- The rights of individuals (access, correction, erasure, appeal to the Authority — Part II), the rules on marketing messages (Part IV) and the penalty regime (Part VII) have no start date yet. They will start only when the Minister publishes a further Gazette Order.
- No rules or regulations under the Act have been finalised; the drafts published in 2024 are consultation documents only.

## 5. Traps to avoid in published content

1. The DPA's own pages est.php and Background.php still say the Act is "fully operational from 18 March 2025". That date was repealed. Never copy it.
2. Several commercial compliance pages claim 72-hour breach notification is law. It is in a draft rule only (DRF-02); s.23 leaves the timeframe to rules not yet made.
3. "Penalties up to Rs 10 million" is in the Act (s.38) but Part VII is not in operation and penalties attach to non-compliance with a Part V directive, not directly to a breach of Part I.
4. The one-month response time for data-subject requests (new s.17(1)) belongs to Part II, which is not in operation.
5. Draft regulations still reference pre-amendment section numbers (e.g. s.26(4)); do not cite them as current.

## F. Decisions, determinations and enforcement information

None found. The DPA has stated (CIR-02 para 2.2) that it will only investigate complaints or hear appeals once the governing provisions are in operation; Parts II and VII are not in operation. No published determinations, penalty notices or appeal decisions exist as at the research date.

# Section-by-Section Reference Matrix and Research Plan

Version 1.0 · 31 Oct 2026 · Built from the English text of Act No. 9 of 2022 (ACT-01) and the Amendment Act No. 22 of 2025 (ACT-02).

Status key: IN = in operation · 2027 = in operation from 1 Jan 2027 (GAZ-04) · NO = no commencement date.
Priority key (for website content): P1 = needed for Phase 2 small-business and citizen pathways · P2 = organisational compliance guide · P3 = reference only.
Audience key: C = citizens · S = small business · O = organisations · T = technical/professional.

For every row, Phase 2 must produce: (1) referenced extract, (2) plain English, (3) plain Sinhala, (4) practical meaning, (5) who is affected, (6) example, (7) actions, (8) consequences, (9) commencement status, (10) official links.

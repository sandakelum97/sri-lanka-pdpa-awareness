# Bilingual Legal Glossary — Sri Lanka PDPA (v0.1)

Status: DRAFT — English side sourced from s.56 of Act No. 9 of 2022 as amended; Sinhala terms marked ✅ were read directly from the official Sinhala text of the Amendment Act No. 22 of 2025 or the 2022 Act (page references given) and still require sign-off by a bilingual legal reviewer. Terms marked ⏳ await conversion of the legacy-font 2022 Sinhala Act (OQ-18). Plain-language explanations in both languages are to be drafted in Phase 2 after the terms are confirmed; nothing here is published yet.

Sources: ACT-01 (EN p.47–53 for s.56; SI p.1 preamble), ACT-02 (SI printed pp.9–12 = PDF pp.10–13; EN pp.8–11).
Abbreviations: Am. = Amendment Act 22/2025; SI p. = printed page of the Sinhala text.

## A. Defined terms (s.56 as amended)

| # | English term | Official Sinhala term | Sinhala source | English definition (paraphrased from s.56; exact statutory phrases in quotes) | Section |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | personal data | පෞද්ගලික දත්ත ✅ | Am. SI p.9–10; Act SI p.1 | Any information that can identify a data subject directly or indirectly — by an identifier (name, identification number, financial data, location data, online identifier) or by factors specific to their physical, physiological, genetic, psychological, economic, cultural or social identity. | s.56 |
| 2 | data subject | දත්ත ආයකයා ✅ | Am. SI p.9 (“දත්ත ආයකයාට”, “දත්ත ආයකයාගේ”); Act SI p.1 preamble (“දත්ත ආයකයන්ගේ”) | An identified or identifiable natural person, “alive or deceased”, to whom the personal data relates. Note: the Act expressly covers deceased persons; an heir may exercise rights for ten years after death (s.17(5)(d)). | s.56, s.17(5) |
| 3 | processing | සැකසීම ✅ (පෞද්ගලික දත්ත සැකසීම = processing of personal data) | Am. SI p.9; GE 2498/16 SI (“I වන කොටසේ (පෞද්ගලික දත්ත සැකසීම)”) | Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, preservation, alteration, retrieval, disclosure, transmission, making available, erasure, destruction, consultation, alignment, combination, or logical/arithmetical operations. | s.56 |
| 4 | controller | පාලකවරයා ✅ | Am. SI p.9–10; GE 2498/16 SI (“පාලකවරුන්”) | Any natural or legal person, public authority, public corporation, NGO, agency or other body which alone or jointly determines the purposes and means of processing. | s.56 |
| 5 | processor | දත්ත සකසන්නා ✅ | Am. SI p.10; GE 2498/16 SI (“දත්ත සකසන්නන්”) | A natural or legal person, public authority or other entity which processes personal data on behalf of the controller; must be a separate entity, not under the controller's hierarchical control; excludes internal processing (one department for another; an employee for the employer). Statutory illustration: a hospital storing patient records on Company B's cloud — the hospital is controller, Company B is processor, the hospital's own data scientist is neither. | s.56 |
| 6 | Data Protection Officer (DPO) | දත්ත ආරක්ෂණ නිලධරයා ✅ | Am. SI p.11 (s.14(1) of Am.) | The person designated or appointed under s.20, “and includes a third party who is not directly employed by a controller or processor, but fulfils the responsibilities under section 20(5)” (definition replaced by Am. s.14(1) — outsourcing now expressly permitted). | s.56 as amended |
| 7 | Data Protection Authority / the Authority | දත්ත ආරක්ෂණ අධිකාරිය ✅ / අධිකාරිය ✅ | Act SI p.1 preamble; Am. SI p.9–11 | The Authority established under s.28. | s.56, s.28 |
| 8 | public authority | පොදු අධිකාරිය ✅ | Am. SI p.11–12 (s.14(2) of Am.) | A Ministry, Department, Provincial Council, local authority, or a Ministry or Department of a Provincial Council; **does not include** a public corporation or a company incorporated under the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007 (definition replaced by Am. s.14(2); the 2022 definition also covered statutory bodies and institutions established by written law). | s.56 as amended |
| 9 | public corporation | රජයේ සංස්ථාව ✅ | Am. SI p.12 | Not separately defined in the Act; used in the definitions of controller and public authority. | s.56 |
| 10 | third country | තෙවන රට ✅ | Am. SI p.12 (s.14(3) of Am.) | “any other territory or country other than Sri Lanka” (replaced by Am. s.14(3); the 2022 definition tied it to adequacy prescriptions under s.26). | s.56 as amended |
| 11 | cross-border data flow | දේශ සීමා හරහා පෞද්ගලික දත්ත සංචලනය ✅ | Am. SI p.9–10 (new s.26) | The movement of personal data out of the territory of Sri Lanka for the purpose of processing in a third country. | s.56, s.26 |
| 12 | consent | කැමැත්ත ✅ | Am. SI p.9 (“දත්ත ආයකයා විසින් ප්‍රකාශිතව ම කැමැත්ත ලබා දී ඇත්නම්”) | Any freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous indication, by written declaration or affirmative action, signifying agreement to processing. Conditions in Schedule III. | s.56, Sch III |
| 13 | special categories of personal data | ⏳ | Act SI (legacy font) | Personal data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs; genetic data; biometric data for unique identification; data concerning health; sex life or sexual orientation; data relating to offences, criminal proceedings and convictions; **personal data relating to a child**. | s.56, s.5(b), Sch II |
| 14 | child | ⏳ | Act SI (legacy font) | A natural person below the age of **sixteen** years. | s.56 |
| 15 | personal data breach | ⏳ | Act SI (legacy font) | Any act or omission resulting in accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, personal data transmitted, stored or otherwise processed. | s.56, s.23 |
| 16 | profiling | ⏳ | Act SI (legacy font) | Processing to evaluate, analyse or predict a data subject's performance at work, economic situation, health, personal preferences, interests, credibility, behaviour, habits, location or movements. | s.56, s.24(1)(a) |
| 17 | automated processing | ⏳ | Act SI (legacy font) | Processing that does not involve any manual processing. | s.56, s.18 |
| 18 | anonymise | ⏳ | — | Permanent removal of personal identifiers so the data can no longer be related to an identified or identifiable person. | s.56, s.10 |
| 19 | pseudonymisation | ⏳ | — | Processing so that data cannot be attributed to a data subject without additional information kept separately under technical and organisational measures. | s.56, s.10 |
| 20 | encryption | ⏳ | — | Ciphering or altering data using a mathematical algorithm to make it unintelligible to unauthorised users. | s.56, s.10 |
| 21 | biometric data | ⏳ | — | Personal data from specific technical processing of physical, physiological or behavioural characteristics allowing unique identification, including facial images, fingerprint (dactyloscopic) and iris data. | s.56 |
| 22 | genetic data | ⏳ | — | Personal data relating to inherited or acquired genetic characteristics giving unique information about physiology or health, from analysis of a biological sample. | s.56 |
| 23 | data concerning health | ⏳ | — | Personal data related to the physical or psychological health of a natural person, including any information indicating health status. | s.56 |
| 24 | financial data | ⏳ | — | Identifiers or personal data that identify an account, card or payment instrument, or data on the relationship with a financial institution, financial status, credit history, including remuneration. | s.56 |
| 25 | recipient | ⏳ | — | A natural or legal person, public authority or body to whom personal data is disclosed. | s.56 |
| 26 | third party | ⏳ | — | Anyone other than the data subject, controller, processor and persons under their direct authority who are authorised to process personal data. | s.56 |
| 27 | relevant regulatory or statutory body | නියාමන ආයතනය ✅ (used in new s.26(5): “අදාළ ව්‍යවස්ථාපිත හෝ නියාමන ආයතනය”) | Am. SI p.10 | The regulatory or statutory body which regulates, authorises or supervises the controller, including a supervising Ministry, for ss.26, 27 and 38. | s.56 |
| 28 | Minister | අමාත්‍යවරයා ✅ | Am. SI p.10; GE 2498/16 SI | The Minister assigned the subject of data protection under Article 44 or 45 of the Constitution. | s.56 |
| 29 | local authority | පළාත් පාලන ආයතනය ✅ | Am. SI p.12 | A Municipal Council, Urban Council or Pradeshiya Sabha, or similar body. | s.56 |
| 30 | prescribed | ⏳ (Am. SI p.10 uses “නියම කරනු ලැබිය හැකි” for “as may be prescribed”) | Am. SI p.10 | Prescribed by regulations made under the Act (s.53). | s.56 |
| 31 | written | ⏳ | — | Includes a document written manually or electronically. | s.56 |

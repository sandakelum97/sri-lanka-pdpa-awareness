# Organisational Compliance Guide

*For private companies, banks and financial institutions, hospitals, schools and universities, IT and BPO companies, telecoms, and government institutions. Each topic states the legal requirement (with section and status) and, separately, recommended practice.*

## Sector notes (to be developed; see OQ-10)
- **Banks and financial institutions:** CBSL Financial Consumer Protection Regulations and banking secrecy continue to apply; the PDPA prevails on personal-data protection where inconsistent (s.3).
- **Hospitals and healthcare:** Sch II(g) provides the basis for care by licensed professionals; large-scale health data is a core DPO trigger.
- **Schools and universities:** children's data is special-category; government schools must appoint a DPO now; biometrics/CCTV need DPIAs.
- **IT, BPO and outsourcing:** most are processors (s.22) for local and foreign controllers; cross-border obligations run both ways; foreign clients' laws (e.g. GDPR) apply independently.
- **Telecommunications:** monitoring of telecom networks is a DPIA trigger; s.27 codes/prefixes may be prescribed with TRCSL.
- **Government institutions:** DPA Circular 01/2026 is the operative guidance; DPO mandatory; s.26(4)–(5) categories may be prescribed.

## 12-month readiness plan (recommended)
1. Q4 2026: inventory; gap assessment against Parts I and III; designate DPO/lead; board briefing.
2. Q1 2027: notices live; legal-basis register; retention schedule; processor contracts; breach plan; DPIAs for in-scope systems.
3. Q2 2027: training; subject-request workflow (ahead of Part II); cross-border register; first internal audit.
4. Ongoing: monitor dpa.gov.lk for rules, directives, guidelines and the Part II/IV/VII commencement Orders.

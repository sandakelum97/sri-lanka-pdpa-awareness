# Small Business PDPA Checklist

Educational checklist. It does not certify compliance and is not legal advice. Items marked **[Act]** are legal duties from 1 January 2027 unless noted; items marked *[Practice]* are recommendations.

## A. Know what you hold
- [ ] **[Act s.12]** One-page list of the personal data you keep: customers, staff, suppliers — what, why, where, who sees it, how long.
- [ ] **[Act s.56/Sch II]** Mark anything sensitive: health, religion, ethnicity, criminal, biometric, **children under 16**.

## B. Collect fairly
- [ ] **[Act s.5]** For each data set, the Schedule I reason (contract / legal obligation / legitimate interests / consent).
- [ ] **[Act s.7]** Removed fields you don't need from forms; stopped routine NIC photocopying.
- [ ] **[Act s.11/Sch V]** A short written notice where you collect data (counter card, form footer, website page, booking confirmation) — in Sinhala/Tamil/English as appropriate.
- [ ] **[Act Sch III; s.27 — Part IV not in force]** Separate, un-ticked opt-in for promotional messages; opt-out in every message.

## C. Keep accurately and for the right time
- [ ] **[Act s.8]** A way for people to tell you about changes.
- [ ] **[Act s.9]** A retention period for each data set; statutory minimums respected; disposal done and noted.

## D. Protect
- [ ] **[Act s.10]** Paper locked away; devices PIN/password-locked; staff have their own logins.
- [ ] *[Practice]* Updates on; MFA on email and cloud; backups; old devices wiped before sale; business WhatsApp on a business number.

## E. Other people who handle your data
- [ ] **[Act s.21]** List of processors (courier, payroll, POS, cloud, SMS gateway) with their data-processing terms on file.
- [ ] **[Act s.26]** Note which systems store data outside Sri Lanka; overseas storage mentioned in your notice.

## F. Be ready
- [ ] **[Act s.12(1)(f), s.23]** A one-page breach procedure: contain, record, assess harm, notify the Authority (once rules set the mechanics), tell affected people if needed.
- [ ] **[Act s.12(1)(h); Part II not in force]** A simple way to receive and log customer requests.
- [ ] **[Act s.24]** If you run CCTV at scale or profile customers: a written impact assessment.
- [ ] **[Act s.20]** Checked whether a DPO is required (for most small businesses: no, thresholds not yet prescribed).
- [ ] *[Practice]* Staff briefed; checklist reviewed every six months; dpa.gov.lk/guidelines.php checked for new rules.

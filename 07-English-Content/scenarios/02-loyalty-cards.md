## The situation

A supermarket chain issues loyalty cards that record name, NIC or phone number, and every purchase, then analyses shopping patterns and sends targeted offers.

## What the Act says

| Provision | What it requires here | Status |
|---|---|---|
| s.5 + Sch I | Points administration: contract (Sch I(b)). Profiling for offers: consent or legitimate interests (Sch I(f)) — but marketing messages themselves need consent (s.27). | 2027 (s.27: NO) |
| s.6 — Purpose | Purchase history collected for points cannot be re-used for an incompatible purpose (e.g. sold to an insurer) without a new basis. | 2027 |
| s.7 — Minimisation | Do you need the NIC number, or is a phone number enough? | 2027 |
| s.11 + Sch V — Transparency | Notice must mention profiling/automated decision-making where used (Sch V item 1(m)) and any sharing. | 2027 |
| s.12 — DPMP | A chain of this size needs a documented programme scaled to its processing. | 2027 |
| s.20 — DPO | Large-scale, systematic monitoring of customers is exactly what s.20(1)(b)(i) targets — thresholds not yet prescribed; plan for a DPO. | 2027 (thresholds pending) |
| s.24 — DPIA | 'Systematic and extensive evaluation ... including profiling' requires a DPIA before processing. | 2027 (form not prescribed) |
| s.18 — Automated decisions | Customers may request review of solely automated decisions with lasting impact. | NO |

Status key: **IN** = in force now · **2027** = in force from 1 January 2027 (Gazette 2498/16) · **NO** = not in force, no date set (Gazette 2427/34 repealed the 2025 date). Links go to the section explainers in `07-English-Content/sections/`.

## What this means for you

### Now (before 1 January 2027)
Audit what the card collects and what the analytics do with it; identify profiling.

### From 1 January 2027
Written notice covering profiling and sharing; DPIA for the analytics; DPMP; likely DPO; retention schedule for purchase history.

### When Parts II, IV and VII commence (no date yet)
Consent-only marketing; customer rights to access and object (s.14(2) against legitimate-interest profiling); penalties.

## Common misunderstandings

- 'We own the data because customers signed up.' — Signing up gives a basis for the stated purposes only (s.6).

## Worked example

A supermarket's notice says: points, personalised offers (with opt-out), and anonymised sales analytics. It drops the NIC field, keeps history for two years, completes a DPIA for its recommendation engine, and names a DPO.

## Good practice (not required by the Act)

These are recommendations from the project, not legal requirements, and are labelled as such on the site.

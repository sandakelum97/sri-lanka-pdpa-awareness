## The situation

A restaurant records names, phone numbers, party size, dietary needs and sometimes special occasions for bookings, in a notebook or an app.

## What the Act says

| Provision | What it requires here | Status |
|---|---|---|
| s.5 + Sch I(b) | Taking a reservation is contract necessity. | 2027 |
| s.5 + Sch II | Dietary needs can reveal health (allergies) or religion (halal, vegetarian for religious reasons) — special categories; consent or Sch II(c)/(g) grounds. Collect only what the kitchen needs. | 2027 |
| s.9 — Retention | Delete booking details after the booking unless the guest asks to be remembered. | 2027 |
| s.10 — Security | Booking app logins, the paper book not left on the counter. | 2027 |
| s.21 — Processors | A third-party booking platform is a processor; check its terms. | 2027 |
| s.26 — Cross-border | Booking platforms usually host abroad; cross-border rules apply. | 2027 (directive pending) |
| s.27 — Marketing | Birthday or event promotions need consent. | NO |

Status key: **IN** = in force now · **2027** = in force from 1 January 2027 (Gazette 2498/16) · **NO** = not in force, no date set (Gazette 2427/34 repealed the 2025 date). Links go to the section explainers in `07-English-Content/sections/`.

## What this means for you

### Now (before 1 January 2027)
Clean out old bookings; check the booking app's terms.

### From 1 January 2027
Treat dietary/health notes as sensitive; short retention; notice (a line on the booking confirmation).

### When Parts II, IV and VII commence (no date yet)
Promotions need consent; guests can ask for their records.

## Common misunderstandings

- 'Dietary preferences are harmless.' — They can be health or religious data (special categories) under s.56.

## Worked example

A café keeps bookings in a shared app. It deletes bookings 30 days after the date, records allergies only when the guest volunteers them, and adds 'We use [app] to manage bookings; details are deleted after 30 days' to its confirmation SMS.

## Good practice (not required by the Act)

These are recommendations from the project, not legal requirements, and are labelled as such on the site.

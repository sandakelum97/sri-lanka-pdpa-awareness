## The situation

A tuition class keeps students' names, ages, school, grades, attendance, parents' numbers and payments — often on a phone, in Excel, or on WhatsApp.

## What the Act says

| Provision | What it requires here | Status |
|---|---|---|
| s.56 — Special categories | Personal data relating to a child (under 16) is a *special category* of personal data. | IN (definition) / applied 2027 |
| s.5 + Sch II(a) | Processing children's data on consent requires the **parent's or guardian's** consent. | 2027 |
| s.7, s.9 | Collect only what teaching requires; delete when the student leaves (subject to any record-keeping law). | 2027 |
| s.10 — Security | Protect the device; avoid posting class lists or results in open groups. | 2027 |
| s.11 + Sch V | Written notice to parents (an enrolment form paragraph). | 2027 |
| s.26 — Cross-border | WhatsApp, Google Sheets and cloud storage are cross-border processing. | 2027 (directive pending) |
| s.20 — DPO | Special-category processing 'on a scale as may be prescribed' — a single class is far below any plausible threshold; a large tuition institute may not be. | 2027 (thresholds pending) |
| ss.13–17 — Rights | Parents may exercise the child's rights (s.17(5)(a)). | NO |

Status key: **IN** = in force now · **2027** = in force from 1 January 2027 (Gazette 2498/16) · **NO** = not in force, no date set (Gazette 2427/34 repealed the 2025 date). Links go to the section explainers in `07-English-Content/sections/`.

## What this means for you

### Now (before 1 January 2027)
Move away from posting results publicly; think about what is on the phone and who else can see it.

### From 1 January 2027
Parental consent where consent is the basis; a short notice on the enrolment form; delete leavers' data; secure the device.

### When Parts II, IV and VII commence (no date yet)
Parents can request access/correction; penalties live.

## Common misunderstandings

- 'Children's data is just like any other.' — No: it is a special category with parental consent and a stronger Sch II basis required.

## Worked example

A maths teacher adds to her enrolment form: who she is, that she keeps attendance, grades and a parent's number to run the class, that records are deleted one year after the student leaves, and that she will not share them. Results go to each parent individually, not to the class group.

## Good practice (not required by the Act)

These are recommendations from the project, not legal requirements, and are labelled as such on the site.

## The situation

A school holds admissions files, health forms, disciplinary records, photos, CCTV, biometric attendance, and shares data with the Ministry, exam bodies and app providers.

## What the Act says

| Provision | What it requires here | Status |
|---|---|---|
| s.56 / Sch II | All of it is children's data (special category); health, religion and biometrics are doubly sensitive. | 2027 |
| s.3 — Other laws | Education statutes and Ministry circulars continue to apply so far as consistent (s.3 proviso for public authorities). | 2027 |
| s.12 — DPMP | A school needs a documented programme. | 2027 |
| s.20 — DPO | Government schools (Ministry/Department) — mandatory. Private schools — likely under s.20(1)(b)(ii) once thresholds exist. | 2027 |
| s.24 — DPIA | Biometric attendance and CCTV are DPIA triggers (s.24(1)(a)/(b)). | 2027 |
| s.21 — Processors | Learning apps, SMS gateways, photo printers are processors; contracts needed. | 2027 |
| s.26 — Cross-border | Google Classroom, Microsoft Teams etc. are cross-border. | 2027 |

Status key: **IN** = in force now · **2027** = in force from 1 January 2027 (Gazette 2498/16) · **NO** = not in force, no date set (Gazette 2427/34 repealed the 2025 date). Links go to the section explainers in `07-English-Content/sections/`.

## What this means for you

### Now (before 1 January 2027)
Inventory what is held and shared; stop collecting what is not needed (e.g. parents' NIC copies 'for the file').

### From 1 January 2027
DPMP, DPO (government schools now; private schools plan), DPIA for biometrics/CCTV, processor contracts, parental notices.

### When Parts II, IV and VII commence (no date yet)
Parental access/correction/erasure rights; penalties.

## Common misunderstandings

- 'Photos on the school Facebook page are fine because parents like it.' — Photos are personal data of children; consent (parental) is the realistic basis, and withdrawal must be possible.

## Worked example

A private school replaces fingerprint attendance with ID cards after its DPIA found the biometric system disproportionate, and publishes a parent-facing notice in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

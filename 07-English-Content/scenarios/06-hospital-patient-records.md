## The situation

A private hospital or clinic holds patient histories, lab results, images, insurance and payment data, and shares with labs, insurers, referral doctors and cloud EMR vendors.

## What the Act says

| Provision | What it requires here | Status |
|---|---|---|
| s.56 — Special categories | Data concerning health, genetic data, biometric data. | IN (definition) / applied 2027 |
| s.5 + Sch II(g) | Processing for diagnosis, care and health-service management by licensed health professionals is a lawful basis without consent. | 2027 |
| s.8, s.9, s.10 | Accuracy, retention (medical-record retention rules under other laws apply — OQ-11), security with encryption and access controls. | 2027 |
| s.12 — DPMP | Required, substantial. | 2027 |
| s.20 — DPO | Large-scale special-category processing is a core activity — a DPO should be planned now. | 2027 (thresholds pending) |
| s.21, s.22 | Labs, EMR vendors, insurers' processors — contracts and instructions. | 2027 |
| s.23 — Breach notification | Must notify the Authority of breaches (rules on timing pending). | 2027 |
| s.24, s.25(7) | DPIA for systematic evaluation of health data; public-health processing requires consultation with the Authority. | 2027 |
| s.26 — Cross-border | Cloud EMR hosted abroad; telemedicine. | 2027 |
| ss.13–16 | Patient access, correction, limited erasure. | NO |

Status key: **IN** = in force now · **2027** = in force from 1 January 2027 (Gazette 2498/16) · **NO** = not in force, no date set (Gazette 2427/34 repealed the 2025 date). Links go to the section explainers in `07-English-Content/sections/`.

## What this means for you

### Now (before 1 January 2027)
Inventory, vendor review, breach procedure, DPO designation.

### From 1 January 2027
Full Part I and Part III compliance; the Act's own illustration in s.56 (hospital/cloud/processor) applies directly.

### When Parts II, IV and VII commence (no date yet)
Patient rights enforceable; penalties.

## Common misunderstandings

- 'We need the patient's consent for every use.' — Not for care by licensed professionals (Sch II(g)); consent is needed for uses outside care, e.g. marketing.

## Worked example

A hospital moving its EMR to a foreign cloud documents the s.26(3)(b)/contract basis, obtains the vendor's data-protection commitments, completes a DPIA, and appoints a DPO.

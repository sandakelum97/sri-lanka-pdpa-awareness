## The situation

Employers hold NIC copies, bank details, EPF/ETF numbers, medical certificates, performance reviews, disciplinary files and CCTV of staff.

## What the Act says

| Provision | What it requires here | Status |
|---|---|---|
| s.5 + Sch I(b)/(c) | Employment contract and legal obligations (EPF, tax, labour law) are the main bases. | 2027 |
| s.5 + Sch II(b) | Health data in employment and social security contexts has a specific Sch II basis where provided for in written law. | 2027 |
| s.7 — Minimisation | NIC number where a law requires it; NIC *copies* only where needed. | 2027 |
| s.9 — Retention | Statutory retention periods (labour, EPF, tax) apply — OQ-11. | 2027 |
| s.10 — Security | HR files locked; HRIS access controls. | 2027 |
| s.11 — Notice | An employee privacy notice. | 2027 |
| s.24 — DPIA | Systematic monitoring of staff (CCTV, email monitoring) may trigger a DPIA. | 2027 |
| s.26 — Cross-border | Foreign payroll/HRIS systems. | 2027 |
| s.18 — Automated decisions | Automated shortlisting/scoring: review right. | NO |
| ss.13–16 | Employee access to their file; correction. | NO |

Status key: **IN** = in force now · **2027** = in force from 1 January 2027 (Gazette 2498/16) · **NO** = not in force, no date set (Gazette 2427/34 repealed the 2025 date). Links go to the section explainers in `07-English-Content/sections/`.

## What this means for you

### Now (before 1 January 2027)
Review what HR collects at onboarding; stop over-collection; check monitoring practices.

### From 1 January 2027
Employee notice; retention schedule aligned to statutory periods; DPIA for monitoring; processor contracts for payroll/HRIS.

### When Parts II, IV and VII commence (no date yet)
Employee rights; automated-decision review; penalties.

## Common misunderstandings

- 'Employees consented in their contract.' — Consent that is a condition of employment is unlikely to be 'freely given' (Sch III(c)); rely on contract/legal obligation instead.

## Worked example

A BPO stops photocopying NICs for every applicant, records the number only for those hired, and issues a one-page staff privacy notice.

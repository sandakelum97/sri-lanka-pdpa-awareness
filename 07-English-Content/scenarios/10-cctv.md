# A CCTV system recording customers

## The situation

A shop, restaurant, school, office or apartment block records customers, visitors and staff on CCTV; footage may be shared with police or posted online after an incident.

## What the Act says

| Provision | What it requires here | Status |
|---|---|---|
| s.56 — Personal data | Identifiable footage is personal data; facial images used for identification are biometric data (special category). | IN (definition) |
| s.5 | Legitimate interests (Sch I(f)) — security, fraud prevention — is the usual basis; posting footage online to shame someone is not. | 2027 |
| s.11 — Notice | Signage telling people cameras operate, who the controller is and the purpose. | 2027 |
| s.9 — Retention | Automatic overwrite after a defined period. | 2027 |
| s.10 — Security | Restricted access to recordings. | 2027 |
| s.24(1)(b) — DPIA | 'Systematic monitoring of publicly accessible areas' requires a DPIA before processing. Whether a two-camera shop is 'systematic monitoring' is an open interpretive question; a mall, campus or city network clearly is. | 2027 (form not prescribed) |
| s.40 / Sch IV | Sharing with police for a lawful investigation has specific grounds. | 2027 (s.40: NO) |
| s.13 — Access | Individuals may request their footage. | NO |

Status key: **IN** = in force now · **2027** = in force from 1 January 2027 (Gazette 2498/16) · **NO** = not in force, no date set (Gazette 2427/34 repealed the 2025 date). Links go to the section explainers in `07-English-Content/sections/`.

## What this means for you

### Now (before 1 January 2027)
Put up signs; set a retention period; restrict who can view.

### From 1 January 2027
Signage, retention, security, DPIA for larger systems; a policy for police requests and for not publishing footage.

### When Parts II, IV and VII commence (no date yet)
Access requests for footage; penalties.

## Common misunderstandings

- 'Posting a shoplifter's clip on Facebook is fine because it's our footage.' — It is the person's personal data; publication needs a lawful basis and is unlikely to be proportionate.

## Worked example

A supermarket posts CCTV notices at entrances, overwrites after 30 days, limits viewing to the manager, and documents a short DPIA.

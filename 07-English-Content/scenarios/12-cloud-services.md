## The situation

Any business using Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Dropbox, Zoho, AWS/Azure, a foreign SaaS CRM, or WhatsApp to hold customer, staff or patient data.

## What the Act says

| Provision | What it requires here | Status |
|---|---|---|
| s.56 — Cross-border data flow / third country | Any territory other than Sri Lanka; storing data on a foreign server is a cross-border flow. | IN (definition) |
| s.26 (as replaced) — Cross-border | Permitted if you keep complying with Parts I–III for that data and adopt instruments the Authority will specify by directive (not issued); or if an exception applies — explicit informed consent, contract necessity, legal claims, public interest, emergency, transit. | 2027 (directive pending) |
| s.21 — Processors | The cloud provider is a processor; contract with the s.21(1)(b) elements. | 2027 |
| s.10 — Security | Appropriate measures — MFA, access control, encryption at rest are examples of 'appropriate'. | 2027 |
| s.11 + Sch V item 1(h) | Notice must mention cross-border transfer. | 2027 |
| s.26(4)–(5) — Public authorities | Barred from transferring categories the Minister prescribes (none yet). | 2027 |

Status key: **IN** = in force now · **2027** = in force from 1 January 2027 (Gazette 2498/16) · **NO** = not in force, no date set (Gazette 2427/34 repealed the 2025 date). Links go to the section explainers in `07-English-Content/sections/`.

## What this means for you

### Now (before 1 January 2027)
Inventory systems hosted abroad; collect each provider's data-processing terms.

### From 1 January 2027
Rely on contract necessity or explicit consent as applicable; have the provider's binding commitments ready for the directive; notice updated.

### When Parts II, IV and VII commence (no date yet)
Directive on instruments; prescribed categories for public bodies.

## Common misunderstandings

- 'The 2022 Act banned cloud for government.' — It restricted it; the 2025 amendment replaced s.26 and the DPA describes the change as enabling cloud-first adoption (Circular 01/2026 para 4.6).
- 'Foreign hosting is illegal.' — No: it is regulated, not prohibited.

## Worked example

An accounting firm on Microsoft 365 records the data-processing addendum, enables MFA, updates its client notice to mention overseas hosting, and tracks the DPA for the s.26(2) directive.

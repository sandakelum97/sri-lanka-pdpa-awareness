# Section 2 — Application of the Act (scope)

Part —: Preliminary (ss.1–3) · Status: **In force from 1 January 2027**

## 1. What the law says (referenced extract)

> The Act applies to processing of personal data (a) wholly or partly within Sri Lanka; or (b) by a controller or processor who (i) is domiciled or ordinarily resident in Sri Lanka, (ii) is incorporated or established under Sri Lankan law, (iii) offers goods or services to data subjects in Sri Lanka, including with specific targeting, or (iv) specifically monitors the behaviour of data subjects in Sri Lanka, including profiling to make decisions about that behaviour. — s.2(1). The Authority may make rules on what counts as specific targeting or monitoring (s.2(2) — none made). The Act does **not** apply to data processed "purely for personal, domestic or household purposes by an individual", or to data that is not personal data (s.2(3)).

## 2. In plain English

The Act covers everyone who handles personal information in Sri Lanka, every Sri Lankan business or body wherever it processes, and foreign companies that sell to or track people in Sri Lanka. It does not cover your private life — your own phone contacts, family photos, a personal diary.

## 4. What it means in practice

Foreign websites, apps and platforms that target Sri Lankan customers are inside the Act (s.2(1)(b)(iii)–(iv)) even with no office here; enforcement against them is a practical question for the Authority. The household exemption is for individuals acting privately, not for a sole trader's customer list.

## 5. Who is affected

Every controller and processor; foreign businesses targeting Sri Lanka; individuals (exempted for private use only).

## 6. Everyday example

A person keeps a WhatsApp group of friends' numbers — household use, outside the Act. The same person runs a home bakery and keeps a customer order list on the same phone — that list is business processing and inside the Act.

## 7. What to consider doing

- Decide which of your activities are business processing and which are private.
- Foreign suppliers you use: note that they too fall under s.2(1)(b) if they target Sri Lankan data subjects.

## 8. Consequences of not complying

Not a duty-creating section; it determines whether the rest of the Act applies.

## 9. Current legal status

**In force from 1 January 2027.** Appointed by Gazette Extraordinary No. 2498/16 of 22 July 2026 (Order under s.1(3) as substituted by Act No. 22 of 2025). Until that date there is no legal duty under this section.

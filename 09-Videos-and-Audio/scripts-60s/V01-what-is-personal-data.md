# V01 — What is personal data? (55 s)
Source: s.56 · Status: definition in force; duties from 2027

**VO (EN)**
Your name. Your NIC number. Your phone number. Your face, when a shop camera records it. Where your phone says you are. Your bank account. If someone could work out that it's you — that's personal data. Some of it the law guards more tightly: your health, your religion, your ethnicity, your fingerprints — and anything at all about a child under sixteen. Sri Lanka's data protection law is about how businesses, schools, hospitals and government offices handle all of this. Most of its duties start on the first of January 2027. We'll take them one at a time.

**OST**: NIC → name → phone → face → location → bank · "If it can be traced to you, it's personal data" · "Extra protection: health · religion · ethnicity · biometrics · children under 16" · "Duties start 1 Jan 2027"

**Shots**: hands at a pharmacy counter; a phone screen with a map pin; a CCTV lens; a school register — flat, warm, Sri Lankan street textures; no faces of real people.

# V03 — Who is the Data Protection Authority? (50 s)
Source: ss.28–35 · Status: IN

**VO (EN)**
If a company misuses your information in Sri Lanka, who do you go to? The Data Protection Authority — a body set up by the Act in 2023, based at BMICH in Colombo. It has a board, a Director-General, and real powers already: it can investigate a complaint, walk into a business and inspect records, and order the business to stop, to fix the problem, or to pay you compensation. What it can't do yet is fine anyone — the penalty section of the Act has no start date. And it has said it will take up complaints once the duty that was broken is itself in force. Address and contact at dattasaviya.

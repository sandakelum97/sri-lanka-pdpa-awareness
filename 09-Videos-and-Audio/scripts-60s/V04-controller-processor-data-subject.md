# V04 — Three words: controller, processor, data subject (55 s)
Source: s.56 · Status: 2027

**VO (EN)**
Three words, one pharmacy. Kamala buys medicine. She's the data subject — the person the information is about. The pharmacy writes down her name and number and decides what to do with it. That makes the pharmacy the controller. The pharmacy stores its records on a cloud app run by another company, which only does what the pharmacy tells it. That company is the processor. Why does it matter? Because from 2027, the controller carries the duties — and has to put a written contract on its helpers. Pick your word, and you know your job.

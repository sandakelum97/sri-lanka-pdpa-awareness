# V06 — Can a business ask for my NIC? (50 s)
Source: ss.5, 7, 11 · Status: 2027

**VO (EN)**
"NIC eka denna." You hear it everywhere — the phone shop, the gym, the building security desk. Can they ask? The law doesn't ban it. But from January 2027 three things change. One: they need a lawful reason. Two: they can only take what that reason needs — a bank opening your account has a reason; a shop photocopying your NIC to sell you a charger doesn't. Three: they have to tell you why. So from 2027, when someone asks for your NIC, ask back: "What do you need it for?" They'll need an answer.

**OST**: "Lawful reason (s.5)" · "Only what's needed (s.7)" · "Tell you why (s.11)" · "Ask: what do you need it for?"

**Shots**: an NIC card sliding across a counter (generic, no real numbers), a photocopier, a raised hand.

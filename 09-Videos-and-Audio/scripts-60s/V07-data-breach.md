# V07 — A data leak. Now what? (60 s)
Source: ss.56, 23, 35 · Status: 2027 (rules pending)

**VO (EN)**
Your laptop with the customer list is stolen. Or you email the staff salary sheet to the wrong person. That's a personal data breach — the law counts loss and mistakes, not just hackers. What do you do? First, contain it: change passwords, wipe the phone, recall the email. Second, write down what happened. Third, ask whether anyone could be hurt — fraud, embarrassment, a child's details — and if so, tell them and help. Fourth, from January 2027 you must notify the Data Protection Authority. How fast? The rules haven't been written. You'll read "72 hours" online; that's a draft, not the law.

# V08 — CCTV and the law (55 s)
Source: ss.9–11, 24 · Status: 2027

**VO (EN)**
A camera over your till is normal. A customer's face on that recording is personal data. From January 2027: a sign at the door saying cameras are running and who runs them. A set number of days before recordings are overwritten. Only a few people able to view them. And one thing you must not do — post a shoplifter's clip on Facebook. It's their data, and the law won't see shaming as a fair reason. If you run a big system that systematically watches a public area, you'll need a written risk assessment before you switch it on.

**OST**: "Sign at the door" · "Overwrite after X days" · "Limited access" · "Don't post it online" · "Large systems: assessment first (s.24)"

# V10 — Customer data in the cloud and on WhatsApp (55 s)
Source: ss.26, 21 · Status: 2027 (directive pending)

**VO (EN)**
Your orders come in on WhatsApp. Your accounts live in Google Drive. Your CRM is American. Is that allowed? Yes. Sending data outside Sri Lanka is regulated, not banned — and the 2025 amendment was written partly to let both business and government use the cloud. From January 2027: keep following the Act for that data; keep your provider's data-protection terms on file; and use whatever safeguard the Authority eventually specifies — it hasn't yet. Practical tip: a business WhatsApp number, not your personal one, and don't forward customer details into group chats.

**OST**: "Cross-border = any server outside Sri Lanka" · "Regulated, not banned (s.26)" · "Keep provider terms (s.21)" · "Business number, no group forwards"

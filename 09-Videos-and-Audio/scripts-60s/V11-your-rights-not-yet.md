# V11 — Your rights. Not yet. (60 s)
Source: ss.13–19 · Status: NO

**VO (EN)**
Here's what the Act promises you. The right to see what any organisation holds about you. To have mistakes corrected. To take back consent, and to object. To have data deleted — in three specific situations. To get an answer within a month, free. To have a human review a decision a computer made alone. To appeal a refusal to the Authority. Every one of these is written into the law. Not one of them is in force, and no date has been set. You can still ask — many organisations will answer. Keep a copy. And when a date is set, you'll find it at dattasaviya.
